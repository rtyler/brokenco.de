This year I moved my personal and business mail away from Proton to
[mailbox.org](https://mailbox.org) and I could not be happier with the
decision. 

The catalyst was **not** any of the recent self-inflicted problems by Proton's
CEO, it was actually Proton CEO Andy Yen's [cheerleading for the incoming Trump
administration](https://www.reddit.com/r/ProtonMail/comments/1i2nz9v/on_politics_and_proton_a_message_from_andy/)
back in January of 2025. That set a chain of events in motion for me to get the
migration planned and executed earlier this year, but my frustrations had been
building for years. 

Proton insists that in order to offer "secure" email, you must use their
non-standards compliant software. Their slow web application. Their slow
Electron-based desktop software. Their slow Android application. If you want to
use another mail client you have to use their slow, and _buggy_ Bridge
application. If you care about email they are actually a **terrible** choice
because they seem to _hate_ the technologies that make email work.

Over many years I _tolerated_ this shitty stack because I believed the
marketing that Proton was a _good_ company. That was until their CEO helpfully
exhibited the rot within their leadership culture.

This week's reporting from 404 Media has made me feel even more vindicated in
leaving. The first article detailing how [Proton is "re-interviewing" staff about
AI](https://www.404media.co/proton-to-re-interview-staff-about-ai-to-see-who-gets-to-keep-their-job/)
to decide who gets to keep their job:

> “These re-interviews are consequential,” a leaked email seen by 404 Media
> reads.

Followed by the [manifestos
piece](https://www.404media.co/manifestos-fear-and-loser-thinking-inside-protons-ai-reckoning/):

> Proton CEO Andy Yen projects a calm persona and rails against Big Tech and
> critiques AI. Internally, he is pushing engineers to build Proton with AI,
> even when they have security concerns.

The toxic culture from Andy Yen described in those reports does not surprise me
but _is_ disappointing for the staff who likely believe in the mission and
marketing as I had.

---

In contrast, [mailbox.org](https://mailbox.org) is a **mail service**. They
understand that their job is to provide mail, and they do it with boring,
reliable, standards-compliant IMAP and SMTP. No forced apps, no Electron, no
lock-in.

I am back to using [K-9
Mail](https://github.com/thunderbird/thunderbird-android) on my phone,
[neomutt](https://neomutt.org/) in the terminal, and
[mbsync](https://isync.sourceforge.io/) to keep everything in sync. It is quite
a breath of fresh air, especially with the visible AI psychosis at my former
mail provider.
